# Monthly publication task template

This is a public template for producing an OpenZcash Monthly reading set. It is not evidence that a scheduled task is active or that publication has been authorized. Keep task identity, saved prompt, deployment configuration and approvals outside this repository.

Read `reports/monthly/OPENZCASH-MONTHLY-PROMPT.md`, `reading-set-contract.json`, `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, `reference.json`, `README.md` and `PUBLISHING.md`. Run `check_monthly_standard.py` with the intended first-of-month occurrence to verify the format, reference PDFs and calendar. Derive the reporting and comparison months from that occurrence; never reuse facts or sign-off from a reference edition.

Prepare exactly two English PDFs, **Monthly** and **Operations**, with **Sources & Data** as the third view. Monthly has the ten page roles and wallet order defined in `reading-set-contract.json`. Operations follows the current evidence rather than a fixed page count. Distinguish requested, approved, committed and paid amounts, and separate source dates from publication dates.

Refresh official sources and reconcile the complete reading set. Review every page, source, number, date, logo and link. Bind the final PDFs, page manifests and source registers to a completed review record. Preserve the financial, source and visual gates; a local preview is not proof of production publication.

Before any push or deployment, verify the live baseline and obtain the required authorization through the private operating process. Use a clean checkout of the verified commit, keep credentials and restricted evidence outside public files, and confirm the served reader and asset hashes after deployment. If a critical source, review, compatibility check or authorization is missing, leave the existing edition live and report the blocker. Do not send messages to third parties without authorization.
