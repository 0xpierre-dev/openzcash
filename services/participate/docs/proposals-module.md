## Participation

Anyone can read published records. Any **active permanent account**, including an account with a passkey, can like and comment without joining the DAO. Temporary ballot invitation sessions do not become public-record accounts. Owners and managers of the record's economic scope moderate comments; committee records use committee assignments rather than the hosting DAO's leadership. Global roles, purchases and public participation grant no DAO authority.

The imported ZCG committee positions and rationale remain historical source material. New local likes, comments and their authors are separate. The cards count active proposal likes and visible comments, including replies. Hidden comments stay in the discussion's audit history and are excluded from the visible-comment count.

Cards start folded. **Comments** and **Read & verify** open and close beneath the same card without changing pages. Committee feedback remains available on hover, focus or tap. Reading and file verification are optional disclosures; standalone record URLs continue to work.

The private balance stays inside **Credits**, accessible from the header and composer. The initial listing has no balance counter or economy banner. The like button discloses its initial cost; the comment composer shows its price before publication. Folding the discussion preserves an unsubmitted draft. Reopening refreshes comments, permissions and evidence. Guests can read both panels and sign in inline. Prices are configurable by the economic scope’s administrator; the current preview uses 1 demo Zcred per comment and initial like.

## Ledger and payment boundaries

- ZCG records use a **COMMITTEE** scope with their own account, policy, receiving-wallet configuration and append-only journal. DAO discussions use their DAO scope. A proposal does not get a separate wallet or balance, and no fallback or transfer between committee and DAO balances is allowed.
- Comment publication and its debit commit atomically. Invalid parents, insufficient balance, price changes or a failed insert leave both unchanged.
- Each proposal or comment has one active like per account. The first paid like costs the displayed price. Unlike is free, and restoring a previously paid like is free. Removing or moderating content does not refund credits.
- Request keys survive uncertain retries. A replay returns current state, never reapplies an obsolete like state or creates another debit. Prior-charge markers and request history cannot be erased.
- Public endpoints expose aggregate counts and the current account's state. Other users' balances and reactor identities remain private. Writes require an active account, trusted origin and validated input, with existing rate limits.
- Credits do not buy votes, DAO membership, committee support or treasury allocations. No author payout, ZEC transfer or automatic sustainability allocation is generated by consuming credits.

The funding model stays **personal wallet → confirmed payment → Zcred**, paid into the selected committee’s or DAO’s configured receiving wallet. The local preview uses the existing fixed demo allowance and keeps real top-ups disabled. This change enables no real payment, broadcast or production deployment.
